# Feasibility claims and external confirmation design

This package freezes the research question, claim scopes, engineering contract,
comparisons, endpoints and failure rules for an external-benchmark confirmation.
It does not run that confirmation. The source-specific asset and adapter freeze
is still required; `register --verify` reproduces this design-stage
`blocked_before_outcomes` state. A later source freeze is a separate artifact,
not a rewrite of this record. A Git design record is not a claim of
formal third-party preregistration.

The question is **when a protected distortion-repayment commitment can be
certified, ruled out or left unresolved, and whether its ideal deadline credit
survives measured software playback**. A new superior recovery controller is
not the contribution being tested. Prior conformal/CVaR streaming, compensation
queues, geometric enumeration and robust dynamic programming remain credited
in the [focused comparison](../player_timing/PRIOR_ART.md) and
[earlier references](../feasibility_synthesis/RELATED_WORK.md).

## Claim and evidence map

| Claim | What would support it externally | What would not |
|---|---|---|
| Finite-contract rejection | Valid optimistic upper below target, checked against independent ledgers/oracle | Absence of a sufficient lower certificate |
| Useful geometric tightening | Additional correct rejection over the convex envelope | Narrower intervals with identical decisions alone |
| Information gap | Recorded-trace feasible case with a below-target universal-policy upper | Treating recorded feasibility as a universal guarantee |
| Completion-accounting sensitivity | Prespecified measured/ideal credit discrepancies, including their absence | Choosing a near-deadline case after seeing timings |
| Delivery sensitivity | Matched fixed-parent paths, separately retained parent/chunk accounts and byte budgets | Claiming optimality or impossibility for shorter adaptive actions |

The six machine-readable claims in `claims.json` bind each statement to existing
sources and required scope qualifiers. These checks guard declared structure;
they do not prove arbitrary prose, a mathematical theorem or journal novelty.
The current development evidence is included in the registration only as
development evidence, with its original hashes and denominators.

## Fixed primary contract

Keep two users, a 20-second past loss interval followed by four original
approximately ten-second recovery parents, rung0 impairment, rung2 reference,
and recovery rungs2..5. Repay at least half the positive native-MSE-duration
loss by40 seconds from the first recovery request. Credit a complete recipient
parent only, and retain both users' native floors over the entire chosen path.
There is a1.5MB actual-file pair cap per parent and a nonrefilling R4 allowance:
the sum of the four original rung2 pair sizes. Charge positive extra bytes
without savings refunds, all startup, requests, waiting and playback time.

Use the existing 12x6 tile-centre100x100-degree angular window, latitude weights,
one-Hz objective samples and native rounding. This is not a perspective HMD
metric. Sender maps, file sizes, past debt and causally observed past feedback
are allowed inputs; unplayed future poses/quality are evaluation-only. The
primary conditional execution allowance remains0.5s per parent, with ideal
and unbounded cases reported separately. A measured finite batch cannot turn
that conditional allowance into a hard deployment bound.

`design.json` also fixes the legacy six-rung encoding ladder and preparation
rules. The exact original stimulus and its presentation offset must be verified
before reuse. No substituted upload, shortened event, looping of a short clip,
retuned ladder, calibrated speed bound or different repayment threshold is
permitted within this design. Any such change needs a separately versioned
research question, not a patch to make a test pass.

## Source inventory and selection boundary

The [official QoEVAVE Saliency catalogue](https://qoevave.github.io/database/docs/Saliency)
lists14 trace-linked clips. The three published project manifests have exposed12.
The remaining ForestWalk02 and Skateboarding05 are alternate takes of families
already used. The [scene catalogue](https://qoevave.github.io/database/docs/Scenes)
distinguishes takes from scene families. The executable inventory consequently
finds zero unused scene families; it does not infer new people from unused
filenames or different audio conditions.

Two external candidates are recorded in `catalogue.json`, without opening raw
media or pose values. The [Corbillon et al. institutional record](https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/publication/4178a12f-0320-4467-9e8d-b461236714bd)
describes59 viewers and five70-second test videos
([paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3083187.3083215)). Its duration suits the fixed
60-second interval, but the original dataset landing page was unavailable
through the current fetch and exact paired assets are not yet verified.
The [Wu et al. author page](https://wuchlei-thu.github.io/) describes48 viewers,
18 videos and five categories. It is an alternative, not an automatic fallback:
the experiment conditions, offsets, paired assets and reuse terms require
verification before selecting its test subset. No raw source was downloaded.

Select all original test clips in one frozen source collection that pass the
metadata-only duration/alignment rules. Record every exclusion, including
unavailable media, with its reason before looking at recovery outcomes. Do not
mix prompted and free-viewing experiments silently. Use the first60 seconds of
the original presentation interval, respecting the published source offset.
All source identities with valid timestamps at the fixed quality samples are
eligible; select no identity by viewport behavior. The maximum age of the next
recorded sample is0.1s, an engineering alignment rule rather than a head-motion
guarantee. A directed cycle of sorted eligible IDs gives every identity exactly
one recipient and donor role per clip.

The sampling unit for descriptive generalization is the original clip, not its
policy runs, directed pairs, timestamps or frames. Report per-clip counts and
equal-clip macro summaries; pooled episode counts are secondary. This is a
census of a selected fixed benchmark, not random sampling of all immersive
content. No iid confidence interval or population p-value is assigned. New
source collection does not establish that natural persons are identity-disjoint
across datasets. This work does not recruit new participants.

## Two-stage freeze before outcome access

1. **Design freeze here:** immutable question, numerical contract, endpoints,
   sample rules, timing repetitions and failure rules; synthetic tests only.
2. **Source/adapter freeze:** exact catalogue and clip exclusions, paired asset
   hashes, source presentation offsets, eligible timestamp/ID roster,
   coordinate/projection checks, reuse review, adapter code and tests. Freeze
   this package and its Git revision before deriving pose-dependent quality or
   recovery outcomes. Metadata inspection is not held-out outcome evaluation.

`preflight` refuses unknown/outcome fields, already exposed declarations,
unbound assets, incomplete catalogues, short intervals, stale required samples,
duplicate IDs and unreviewed source selection. Its output is a declaration
check, not verification of permission or scientific truth, and never starts
an experiment. The actual adapter is not implemented in this package. A source
must not be declared compatible merely because the JSON passes.

Missing source fields are a genuine execution gate, not a reason to invent a
complete registration. The current record honestly says design frozen, source
and adapter freeze pending. No selected catalogue or fixed participant count
is falsely presented as ready for an external run.

## Analyses and decision rules

Primary analysis uses the conditional0.5s mode. Compare geometric and convex
envelopes on the same actions, resources and masks; verify every selected path
with an independent scalar ledger and the exact future-known finite oracle.
Report optimistic rejection coverage, added rejections, interval-to-oracle gaps,
and observed-opportunity/universal-promise coexistence. The last comparison
deliberately has different quantifiers: an upper for a universal policy may be
below the credit attainable on a particular recorded trajectory.

All soundness checks must pass. A lower above the observed-trace optimum or an
optimistic rejection of a trace-feasible case invalidates that certificate.
An absent sufficient certificate is abstention, not impossibility. Zero past
debt is reported as no event. A reference service that already violates the
byte cap is reported separately; neither case becomes a recovery success or an
excuse to alter the limits. Missing or invalid episodes retain their registered
denominators. The current pure endpoint summarizer accepts valid positive- or
zero-debt records; missing/reference-infeasible cases must remain in the planned
roster and separate disposition ledger, not be fabricated as complete records.

Report whether geometric tightening changes any external decisions and whether
the information gap appears on any external case. If either count is zero,
state that the corresponding exposed empirical finding did not replicate.
These narrow presence checks are not effect-size thresholds for journal
acceptance. No minimum recovery-success rate is selected to favor this work.
Zero admissions leaves fulfillment fraction undefined. Neither rejecting all
cases nor abstaining on all cases establishes a useful positive admission
service. The ordinary Nash/max-min and greedy paths are diagnostic controls,
not reproduced Risk as a Resource, DK-MARC or FairEdge360 implementations.

## Prespecified software timing replication

For each selected clip, use the first directed pair, three frozen path rules,
two delivery-unit sizes and two startup modes, with three repetitions. This is
36main sessions per clip, plus two stress controls after each repetition on the
first sorted clip. Rotate the12 cells by clip index plus repetition index and
run sequentially. A timeout or invalid session is retained; there are no
automatic retries or post-result replacement clips. Report every repetition
and the per-clip median paired contrasts without treating repeats as people.

Preserve the original parent account and a separately derived chunk account;
hold each parent rung across all its children. The main endpoints are signed
measured-minus-ideal credited fraction, protected-target disagreement,
completion time and invalid/missing event accounts. Do not choose cases near
40s after observing them. Zero disagreement is an admissible result. Browser
callback/ended/report timing remains software evidence, with separate server
and client clocks and no physical-display or human-QoE claim. Reuse the original
150s watchdog and two stress controls; do not adjust them after failures.

## Reproduction and status

```sh
python -m unittest confirmation_protocol.test_protocol -v
python -m confirmation_protocol.register --verify
python -m confirmation_protocol.register --check-manifest path/to/source_freeze.json
```

Only the first creation uses `--freeze`; an existing destination is refused.
Source hashes bind this design and the existing evidence. The register does
not rerun earlier experiments, access future data, alter Overleaf or submit a
manuscript. Frozen historical packages and the acknowledgment are unchanged.
