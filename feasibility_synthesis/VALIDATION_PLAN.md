## Recommended next substantive study

**Exposed-data timing generalization with a timestamped software player**, not
another recovery-controller tuning sweep and not a fresh holdout yet.

The purpose is to test whether the management diagnosis is a consequence of
the current cold-start, approximately ten-second, whole-segment commitment
design. A proposal should specify before execution:

1. The original configuration as a locked reference. Any secondary 1/2/5-second
   segment variants must use real valid encodes and explicitly different action
   contracts, not copied ten-second quality labels or silent replacement of
   the original result. Charge the same physical-time budget; do not grant a
   fresh reserve every time a shorter segment is requested.
2. Cold and declared prebuffered starts, with startup cost and advance-fetched
   bytes accounted for. Choose the starting ledger consistently; do not give
   free historical quality or prefetch capacity to one policy.
3. A common monotonic clock recording request, receipt, decode, renderer
   presentation callback and report availability. Record scheduling delay,
   missing/reordered reports and callback uncertainty. If actual presentation
   cannot be measured, keep a software-event label rather than claiming pixels.
4. Matching cost-greedy, rolling Nash and max-min information and resources,
   alongside offline bounds. The primary question is the opportunity and
   promise gap, not whether a selected controller beats all baselines.
5. Separate idealized service from controlled measured service, including
   competing clients and violated-contract negative controls. Do not infer
   spare capacity from application usage. No hard execution bound follows
   merely because a finite batch satisfies an allowance.

No numerical superiority threshold is chosen after seeing this synthesis.
The next protocol should first define measurement validity and the primary
question: does the qualitative feasibility/information gap persist, narrow,
or disappear as commitment and startup assumptions change? Each outcome must
be reportable. If it disappears, narrow the claim to the original design.

## Before any fresh confirmation

Freeze the final research question, information/action contracts, primary
outcomes and dependence-aware analysis. Inventory genuinely unexposed content
and participant identities; do not reuse held-out data that already informed
these audits. Confirm the feasibility/measurement result, not a retrospectively
selected controller. Count independent scenes/people, not policy runs or frames.

For a two-user paper, retain that scope. A broader multi-user title requires
separate scalability/optimality-gap evaluation of the actual new analysis,
not reuse of historical scalability from a different method.

## Before a manuscript rewrite or submission

Resolve the recent closest-work full-text comparisons in `RELATED_WORK.md`.
Map every claim to an evidence kind and an assumption. Retain all adverse
findings and existing disclosures. A framing change cannot by itself establish
novelty, empirical generality or operational benefit. No journal contact or
submission follows automatically from completion of software tests.
