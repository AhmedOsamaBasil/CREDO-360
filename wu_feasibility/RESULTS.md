# Fixed-contract Wu feasibility results

## Outcome and scope

The registered analytical evaluation completed all 16 original clip registrations,
including the six retained metadata exclusions. All 452 original directed pairs
in ten eligible clips were evaluated: 216 free-exploration and 236 content-focused
memory-test pairs, five eligible clips per condition. There were no zero-debt or
reference-cap-infeasible pairs in this eligible census. No controller was fitted,
no contract was changed and no new media or playback experiment was run.

The results support **decision-level usefulness of geometric rejection bounds**
and **a gap between recorded-trajectory opportunity and universal online promises**
under the fixed model. They do **not** establish useful positive certified
admission or a superior recovery controller: the certificate admitted no pair.

policy upper bound quantifies a worst-case guarantee over unrestricted sampled
geometric futures, not the success probability of real users or the impossibility
of succeeding on an individual observed trajectory.

## Frozen causal controls

These are offline analytical evaluations of fixed past-only controls under the
same completion model, not new deployable-controller measurements. All their
positive primary admissions occur in the focused condition; no control admits
a free-exploration pair. The reference path is executed after nonadmission.

| Control | Focused admissions | Fulfilled | False promises |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Point planner | 48 | 45 | 3 |
| Cost-greedy | 48 | 47 | 1 |
| Rolling Nash | 40 | 35 | 5 |
| Max-min | 40 | 35 | 5 |

Rolling Nash and max-min have exactly identical primary decisions and executed
paths in all 452 pairs; they are not independent corroboration. Point and greedy
have identical admission decisions, but differ in 48 executed paths. Greedy and
rolling Nash match on 404 executed paths and 444 decisions. The numerical control
ordering is descriptive and not a claim of statistically established superiority.
The geometric-gated control variants admit no pair. Reject-all/abstain-all are
retained diagnostic decisions, not useful positive admission services.

The saved per-pair records also contain the secondary ideal-execution results,
unbounded-execution certificate, uncapped native credit bounds, normalized gaps,
both users' floor violations, proposed/executed paths and complete chosen-path
ledgers. The primary findings are not selected from alternative timing models.

## Verification and retained platform discrepancy

Both systems pass the registered scientific checks, including:

- 904 independent scalar observed-trace oracle checks;
- 9,944 independent selected-path resource/floor/completion accounts;
- 14,720 independent resource-path clock comparisons;
- 40 scalar certificate-envelope checks and 20 Bellman/exhaustive comparisons;
- containment of all 32,544 integer quality differences in geometric bounds.

No bound soundness, conservation, causal-input or independent-accounting failure
was observed. The Windows result reproduces exactly on Windows. Strict Linux
replay **failed** at e1v05 due to an extremal witness identifier; the complete
Linux diagnostic subsequently retained four different *maximum-mask IDs*:
one in e1v05 and three in e2v01. Every bound, native reward, causal-control path,
decision, ledger and summary is exactly equal. Every platform-specific witness
passes the original independent scalar-extremum check. No minimum-mask adversary
response changes. The full Windows and Linux artifacts and exact differences are
published; they are **not byte-identical**.

The registered evaluator remains unchanged. A disclosed post-freeze audit checks
exact equality of all non-witness fields and reruns the original scalar checks.
It does not replace the original failed strict replay with a claim of exact
cross-platform equality or widen any scientific bound/tolerance.
