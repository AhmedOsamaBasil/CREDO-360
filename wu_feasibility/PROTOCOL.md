# Wu fixed-contract feasibility integration v1

This integration applies `confirmation_protocol/design.json` to the complete
`wu_pipeline_v2/results/attempt_01` artifact. The design precedes source
preparation; this integration is frozen **after** the quality labels were
created and exposed, but before computing these feasibility outcomes. This is
not a new untouched holdout or formal external preregistration. No controller
is fitted, and no prior method, source, exclusion, contract or ladder is changed.

## Fixed data and information boundary

Retain all 16 registrations, the six original exclusions, ten qualified clips
and 452 original directed pairs. Keep free exploration and memory-test viewing
conditions separate. Repeated source IDs are not independent new participants.
Use only hash-bound public derived maps, offline parent labels and measured
parent file sizes/durations; do not open original videos, poses or demographics.
Child labels are not used. The phase diagnostic remains part of interpretation:
native distortion includes resampling and compression, and the angular-window
metric is neither a perspective HMD render nor subjective QoE.

Sender maps and costs may be known in advance. Planning receives only completed
two-parent entry data (positive native-MSE debt, past reference distortion and
past experienced quality); future user labels are supplied only to evaluators.
Policies are the frozen point planner, cost-greedy, rolling Nash and max-min,
plus geometric certificates and reject-all/abstain-all diagnostics. No new
predictor, feedback model, controller, bandwidth or service contract is selected.

## Arithmetic and methods

The original 50% target, 40-second deadline, rung-2 floors including late
parents, 1,500,000-byte pair cap, R4 reserve, 150,000-byte/s fluid service and
0.13-second setup remain unchanged. Durations and full served file sizes come
from the audited manifest. Conditional 0.5-second per-parent execution is
primary; zero allowance is secondary; unbounded execution cannot certify a
positive admission. These clocks are analytical, not new physical measurements.

Reuse the frozen convex/geometric envelopes, exact resource-path enumeration,
clock, certificate, forecast and policy routines. The geometric family has 216
masks, independently selectable at each of ten samples per parent. Verify
extrema independently and retain the original outward two-micro-unit padding.
Use Wu's separately rounded **negative** native-MSE units (nearest-even).
The older `adaptive_audit.response_table` uses half-up positive units, so this
integration implements the same fixed minimizing-mask response with the Wu
rounding convention, independently checked with scalar cosine sums and `round`.
No historical file is edited. Exact integer disagreements fail the batch.
Past PSNR inputs to the frozen fairness controls are obtained using the existing
`admission_contract.core.psnr_units` conversion of native integer units. Native
MSE, not dB, defines all primary debt, credit and floor outcomes.

For each resource-feasible clip, enumerate all finite paths and compare their
resource/clock ledgers with an independent scalar enumeration. Compute exact
observed-trace maxima and check them by scalar accumulation over that independently
constructed ledger. Compare geometric and convex certificates, and bound all
deterministic causal policies by the fixed action-conditioned mask adversary;
relaxing quality floors only in that upper bound makes it optimistic. Verify its
Bellman maximum against complete path enumeration. It is not an exact robust
optimum, and neither known-trace feasibility nor this bound implies online success.

The predeclared endpoint checker uses the maximum safe geometric lower credit.
Its witness is therefore a **lower-maximizing** path. Separately retain and check
the operational certificate's minimum-cost target-reaching path; these witnesses
need not coincide. Save uncapped bound/witness credits in native micro-MSE ticks;
reported repayment is capped at debt. No-event cases have no fulfilled recovery.

Every registered pair is retained. A recovery reference pair above the fixed cap
is `reference_infeasible`, even if a different rung happens to fit; no policy
is launched for that service contract. Record zero debt independently. A zero
past reference/spherical distortion invalidates only the frozen point forecast;
record that disposition rather than add an epsilon or alter the predictor.
Unexpected arithmetic, containment, source, accounting or endpoint failures stop
the batch and retain all completed clip checkpoints and a failure record.
No overwrite, resume, automatic retry, outcome-driven exclusions or substitutions.

## Reporting and decision rules

Primary endpoints and their interpretation are unchanged: soundness violations;
additional geometric versus convex optimistic rejections; observed opportunity
coexisting with a universal-promise upper bound below target; admit/impossible/
abstain counts; absolute and positive-debt-normalized bound gaps. Compute per-clip
counts and equal-clip macro fractions separately within each viewing condition.
Show reference-infeasible/no-event counts and the contributing denominator for
every fraction. Pooled episode counts are descriptive secondary numbers, never
iid trials, p-values or confidence intervals. The original endpoint checker is
called only for positive/zero-debt reference-feasible pairs; service-infeasible
pairs remain in the outer census, not disguised as missing outcomes.

Point/control admissions follow the original predicted-feasibility gate; a
nonadmission executes the reference path. Retain proposed and executed paths,
predicted and actual results, and geometric-gated versions. Report identical
executed paths as equivalence, not independent corroboration. Reject-all is a
diagnostic decision, not a valid proof of impossibility on feasible cases.
Zero admissions leave fulfillment undefined. Zero additional rejections imply
no decision-level improvement here. Zero opportunity/promise gaps leave that
empirical coexistence unconfirmed. Passing does not imply journal readiness.

The predeclared browser completion experiment is a **later separate stage**.
This batch does not run it, change the manuscript or establish a hard execution
bound. Publish the method before evaluation, then publish all results and checks.
